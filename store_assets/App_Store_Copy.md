## Full Description (App Store / Google Play)

**Meet NutriSense AI — Your Personal AI Nutritionist and Kitchen Assistant.**

Tired of manually searching for every single ingredient you eat? NutriSense AI revolutionizes how you track your nutrition, manage your kitchen, and plan your meals. Powered by advanced AI vision technology, achieving your health goals has never been this effortless.

**📸 AI Food Camera**
Simply point your camera at your plate. Our advanced AI instantly identifies the food, estimates the serving size, and breaks down the exact calories, protein, carbs, and fats. It even detects hidden ingredients and cross-checks them against your personal dietary restrictions and allergies.

**🧊 Smart Fridge Inventory**
Stop throwing away expired food. Scan your groceries as you unpack them, and NutriSense AI will automatically build your digital pantry. We track estimated expiration dates and categorize your food, so you always know exactly what's fresh and what needs to be eaten soon.

**🍽️ Zero-Waste Recipe Generator**
Don't know what to cook? Tell NutriSense AI to look in your Smart Fridge. With one tap, generate three unique, chef-inspired recipes using only the ingredients you already have. Every recipe includes prep times, macro breakdowns, step-by-step instructions, and a zero-waste tip.

**📊 Comprehensive Daily Dashboards**
Visualize your progress with beautiful, intuitive daily dashboards. Track your macros through visual rings, monitor your hydration, and get a complete breakdown of micronutrients like sodium, fiber, and vitamins. 

**⚡ Smart Copy & Barcode Scanner**
Eat the same thing often? Use Smart Copy to instantly duplicate yesterday's meals. For packaged goods, our lightning-fast barcode scanner connects to the Open Food Facts database for instant, accurate nutritional profiles.
